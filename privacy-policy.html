Privacy Policy

We believe privacy should be understandable, not hidden behind complicated language. This policy explains what information Novariyan may collect through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Novariyan ("Novariyan", "we", "us" or "our").

Novariyan provides digital design, website development, e-commerce experiences, AI-assisted workflows and related digital services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us or submit an enquiry.

  • Your name
  • Your business or organisation name
  • Your industry
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your indicative project budget
  • Information contained in your project message
  • Information you provide when communicating with us

Our website enquiry form currently prepares an enquiry message for WhatsApp rather than storing the submitted form information on our own website server.

3. Information collected automatically

Like many websites, basic technical information may be processed when you visit the website. Depending on the services enabled on the website, this may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate visit time
  • Referring website

We only enable additional analytics or marketing technologies where they are configured on the website and, where required, after obtaining the appropriate consent.

4. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Discuss potential projects
  • Provide requested services
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Maintain website security
  • Understand website performance where analytics are enabled
  • Meet applicable legal or administrative requirements

5. WhatsApp enquiries

Our website may open WhatsApp when you submit an enquiry or choose to contact us using the WhatsApp buttons.

Once you leave our website and use WhatsApp, information is handled according to WhatsApp's own privacy practices and terms.

Please avoid sending passwords, financial account information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through a general project enquiry.

6. Cookies

The current website code stores your cookie preference in your browser's local storage; it does not use a cookie for that preference. No analytics or marketing technologies are currently loaded by this website code.

The password-protected site manager uses a necessary, HttpOnly, same-site session cookie to keep an administrator signed in. It is marked Secure on HTTPS and is not used to track visitors.

You can change your cookie preferences using the Cookie Settings option provided on the website.

7. Third-party services

The website may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Fonts
  • Unsplash, which provides website photography
  • WhatsApp
  • Website hosting providers
  • Analytics services, if enabled
  • Other tools required to operate or improve the website

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, resolve enquiries, maintain business records or comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Request information about how your data is handled

11. Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses and general audiences. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or legal obligations.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
